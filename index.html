A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

A little Snake 101.

Guide the snake to the orange snacks. Each snack earns 10 points and makes your snake a little longer. Hit a wall or your tail and the round ends.

Move
Arrow keys, WASD, swipe, or on-screen arrows
Start / pause / resume
Space or the on-screen buttons
Restart
The ↻ button starts a fresh round

Choose Chill, Classic, or Speedy before playing. Your best score is saved separately for each pace in this browser.
